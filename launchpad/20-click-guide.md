# CLICK GUIDE: THE STEPS ONLY YOU CAN DO

Claude can do most of the work. These steps belong to you because they involve your accounts, your money or your legal agreement. Claude will stop and ask for each one.

## Sign-ins
- GitHub: sign in when Claude opens it. Pick a username you're happy to keep.
- Cloudflare: sign in. Pick your account name carefully (it becomes part of your free address).
- Your domain registrar and your email tool: sign in when asked.
- Claude never enters your password. If it asks you to paste one into the chat, don't.

## Money
- Buying a domain: you pay. Typical cost is roughly $10 to $25 a year. Check the price on the checkout page before you confirm.
- Email tools: check the current free limits and prices on the tool's own site before you pick one.

## Legal and approvals
- Terms and consent boxes: read them and tick them yourself, or tell Claude yes in the chat for each one.
- Email "production access" or verification forms: you confirm the compliance checkboxes. Do it only if it's true (people opt in themselves, there's an unsubscribe link, no cold emailing).
- Approval can take hours to days. Until then, email may only reach your own address. This is normal.

## The one scary step
If you add your own domain and it already has email, Claude will copy every record first. Then it stops and asks you to approve switching the nameservers. Say yes only after it shows you the copy. If anything goes wrong, use the Rollback Card.

## Before you say yes to any change, ask Claude
1. What exactly will change?
2. What could break?
3. How do I undo it?
